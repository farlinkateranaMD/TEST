How did I use creativity to deepen understanding or share knowledge?

I integrated visual and spatial learning strategies to translate complex 3D human anatomy and microscopic tissue architecture into intuitive, structured study resources. By designing clear visual representations, anatomical breakdowns, and educational diagrams, I converted abstract physiological concepts into tangible learning models. This creative synthesis enhanced my own long-term retention while producing accessible reference tools that facilitate peer discussion and bridge health literacy gaps when explaining medical concepts.
